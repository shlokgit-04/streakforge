Privacy policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

What StreakForge is

A DSA streak companion. You solve problems, it tracks your streak, heat grid and revision queue. It works fully offline in local-only mode.

Data without an account (local-only mode)

Everything (solves, streak, revision schedule) lives in your browser's localStorage. Nothing is sent anywhere. Clearing browser data clears the streak.

Data with an account

If you create an account, StreakForge stores:

  • Your email, an optional display name, and a salted, hashed password (bcrypt). The plaintext password is never stored.
  • Your solve history (dates and problem slugs) and your spaced-repetition review state, so the same streak and revision queue appear on every device.

No trackers, no analytics, no advertising, no third-party requests beyond the problem links to leetcode.com that you open yourself.

How your data is protected

  • Passwords are hashed with bcrypt before storage.
  • Sign-in tokens are cryptographically signed, expire after 30 days, and are sent only over HTTPS.
  • Request bodies are size-capped, and endpoints are rate-limited to blunt abuse.

Your rights

  • Export: a full copy of your solve history and review state, from the account menu on the main page.
  • Deletion: "Delete account" removes your account and all stored solves and reviews immediately.
  • Local-only mode: you can use StreakForge without an account at any time.
