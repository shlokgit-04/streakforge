About StreakForge

Why it exists

Consistency is the hard part of LeetCode practice. StreakForge removes the three excuses: it picks today's problem (rotating topic), shows the streak on a GitHub-style heat grid, and uses spaced repetition (3, 7, 14, 30 days) to resurface solved problems before they fade.

How it works

  • Offline-first: everything works from local browser storage, cached by a service worker, installable as a PWA.
  • Accounts, optional: sign in to sync the streak across devices. The server merges your local history on first sign-in.
  • Problem bank: 64 curated problems across 9 topics, each linking to the real LeetCode problem.
